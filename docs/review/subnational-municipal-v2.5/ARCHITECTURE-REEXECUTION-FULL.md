## Source hierarchy and non-impersonation

### What controls the re-execution

#### Provenance and precedence

- Charter and `NS-KB-01/02` governance guardrails control institutional independence, clean-room names, and anti-capture.
- Current checked repository `NS-RFC-001..014` controls core RFC identity and protocol semantics; canonical LGA and industry ledgers control identifiers.
- User-supplied v2.4 specification adds 8-dimension targets, module workflows, OpenAPI/JSON Schema candidate structures and source ingestion targets.
- 960-file 934-labelled webcopy ZIP supplies 774 council, 37 state, 32 industry, 14 core/66 extended RFC pages and 22 protocol pages; it is *source webcopy*, not evidence of live deployment.
- November 23 architecture provides the earlier crosswalk and matrix framework; conflicts are explicitly recorded, not silently overwritten.
- External official city program pages and Nigerian law/regulatory sources support comparison or legal caution only; they do not authorize domestic deployments.

### Four-wall institutional boundary

#### Reject capture, but enable interoperable service implementation

NigeriaStack specifies open schemas, reference edge clients, conformance tests and verification patterns. A state, FCT council, independent operator or authorized TSP may implement the actual interface under the correct legal and financial jurisdiction. NigeriaStack does not become the federal/state registry, payment switch, official council, issuing court or centralized super-app.

### Empirical and legal claims

#### Distinct institutional, RFC and implementation states

The institutional charter is ratified. A core RFC is governed by its own publication/lifecycle status. A State/LGA service is assessed only on evidence. All 296 source readiness cells remain Not yet assessed. The fiscal zero-escrow requirement is an architectural choice rather than a quote from s121(3) or the 2024 Supreme Court decision for every municipal fee. The Evidence Act has 2023 amendments and a signed digest cannot guarantee court admissibility. NDPA s37 safeguards apply to significant solely automated decisions, not as an unrestricted legal delegation to AI agents.

### Research decision rule

#### Empirical residual-gap test

A new interoperable primitive becomes an independently validated priority only if at least two organizationally independent adopting authorities have the same still-unsolved cross-provider failure, available national/domestic solutions have been tested fairly, and the candidate contract improves an agreed measurable outcome without transferring legal control, data custody or statutory revenue.

This is a research gate, not a claim of missing software, inadequate Nigerian providers or inferiority to an international stack.

**Required payload keys:** `courtIdentifier`, `matterRef`, `privacyClassification`. **Additional candidate keys:** `courtIdentifier`, `matterRef`, `filingDate`, `privacyClassification`, `judicialDirectionRef`.

**Module-scoped API:** `/v1/municipal/{councilCode}/services/LGA-13`. POST application, GET scoped case, POST staged observation/delta, POST authorized decision and permitted GET public verification. Module-specific JSON Schema: `schemas/modules/LGA-13.schema.json`.

#### Offline HLC state machine and reconciliation

COURT_INTAKE → RESTRICTED_CLASSIFIED → REGISTRAR_REVIEW → DOCUMENT_SEALED → APPEAL_REFERRED → ARCHIVED.

Offline records are staged with HLC and operator attribution; the source authority resolves noncommutative conflicts. An officer cannot bypass trusted time, payment reconciliation or privacy rules by replaying a local CRDT event.

#### Statutory, financial and evidence rejection rules

Council geo code gives no judicial competence; family/juvenile sealed. Council has no judicial write rights; juvenile/family matters quarantined.

**Failure paths:** wrong issuer/tenant → 403; invalid schema → 422; duplicate unresolved operation → 409; restricted public export → 403; clock drift >300s → staged review; unpublished/unauthorized tariff → block payment; signed proof with stale revocation → report stale, not current-valid.

**Trust building blocks:** BB-02, BB-04, BB-05, BB-10. **Gap mechanisms:** MECH-02, MECH-05, MECH-06. **Operational status:** Not yet assessed.
