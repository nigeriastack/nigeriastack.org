# Global City Benchmark to NigeriaStack Functional Crosswalk (Task 1)

## Method and statutory separation

### Functional comparison rather than institutional copying

#### Scope and evidence basis

The eight city groups offer **66 comparative service functions**. Each mapping is a candidate NigeriaStack function with named municipal modules, building blocks and RFCs. The domain counts are research organizing lenses and not audited completeness/rankings of foreign municipal stacks. MyNa Portal and RedeemSG are national; AT HOP belongs to Auckland Transport; Quezon City and Makati are separate municipalities. The comparison therefore does not import their statutory powers or central databases.

NigeriaStack is the charter-ratified independent Nigerian whole-of-economy specification and conformance commons; individual RFCs have their own lifecycle. This comparative matrix does not certify any deployed Nigerian State or council service.
