# **5\. NS-RFC-014: `LgaGovernanceStack` — 774 Constitutional Local Government Areas & 6 FCT Area Councils Municipal Specification**

* **Status:** CANDIDATE STANDARD (Stage 3\)  
* **Working Group:** WG-05 (in joint session with WG-03)  
* **Namespace URI:** `urn:ngstack:lga-stack:v2026`  
* **Statutory Anchor:** Section 7(1) & Fourth Schedule, 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (as amended); Supreme Court Judgment *AG Federation v. AG Abia State & 35 Ors (SC/CV/343/2024, July 11, 2024\)*; Birth, Death, Etc. (Compulsory Registration) Act; Evidence Act 2011 s. 84\.  
* **Target Gap Mechanisms:** `MECH-02` (Grassroots Coverage Gap), `MECH-05` (Forged Paper Certificates), `MECH-06` (Rural Power/2G Drops), `MECH-07` (Cash-Touting / *Agbero* IGR Leakage)

## **5.1 Normative Architecture for the 14 Municipal Service Modules (`LGA-01` to `LGA-14`)**

Every implementation of `LgaGovernanceStack` across Nigeria's 774 LGAs MUST support offline-first `KioskBase` (**\< 35 KB** PWA / POS / USSD) operation across all 14 standardized Fourth-Schedule modules:

* **Citizen & Commercial Licensing Modules:** `LGA-01` (`CouncilComplain`), `LGA-02` (`BuildClear`), `LGA-03` (`WashConnect`), `LGA-04` (`ShopPermit`), `LGA-05` (`VitalProof`), `LGA-06` (`IndigeneProof`), `LGA-07` (`SafetyNOC`), `LGA-08` (`WardWorks`), `LGA-11` (`WardPHC`), `LGA-13` (`CustomaryDocket`), and `LGA-14` (`StreetGrid`).  
* **Direct Revenue & Fiscal Modules:** `LGA-09` (`TenementRate`), `LGA-10` (`MarketCollect`), and `LGA-12` (`CouncilLedger`).

## **5.2 Supreme Court `SC/CV/343/2024` Direct LGA Treasury Invariance**

1. **Zero State Joint Account (`SJLGA`) or Vendor Diversion:** All demand-based field collections (`LGA-10` `MarketCollect` across motor parks, open markets, abattoirs, and loading bays) and property rates (`LGA-09` `TenementRate`) MUST enforce `NS-RFC-009` (`SplitDirect`) with `platform_escrow_amount_kobo == 0` and `state_intermediary_escrow_kobo == 0`.  
2. **100% Direct LGA Treasury Settlement:** Statutory principal sums route directly into the respective Local Government Council's verified CBN/Commercial Bank Treasury Account.  
3. **Anti-Forgery `IndigeneProof` & `VitalProof` Seals:** Every LGA Indigene/Residency Attestation (`LGA-06`) and Birth/Death/Marriage record (`LGA-05`) MUST be signed by the authorized LGA Registrar/Chairman FIDO2 Ed25519 key (`SureEvidence` `s84_` ULID), eliminating forged paper indigene letters nationwide.

## **5.3 Deep-Dive Specification & Playbook Audience/Pain-Point Crosswalk for All 14 Municipal Service Modules — Zero Summarizers**

## **5.4 Normative Grassroots Safeguards & The Five Tests Verification Matrix for NS-RFC-014 (RED-AUDIT-01 to RED-AUDIT-07)**

4. **Inter-LGA Commercial Vehicle Corridor Pass (`LGA-10 MarketCollect` Anti-Double-Taxation Rule):** Any commercial haulage truck, minibus, or agricultural produce vehicle holding an active `SureEvidence` (`s84_` ULID) daily or annual transit emblem MUST be verifiable offline in \< 50ms via 300-DPI QR scan across all participating LGAs along the transit corridor, prohibiting unlawful roadside cash extortion or multiple taxation.  
5. **72-Hour Statutory SLA, USSD/NIN Vouching & Fee-Cap Invariance on `LGA-06 IndigeneProof`:** Every `LGA-06` (`IndigeneProof`) application MUST complete verification or issue a receipted deficiency notice within **72 hours**. Traditional/ward authorities MAY attest origin via zero-data USSD (`*347*LGA#`) bound to their registered NIN/FIDO2 credential, and statutory fees MUST route 100% via `SplitDirect` with zero intermediary surcharge.  
6. **Zero-Smartphone / 2G Feature-Phone Accessibility (`01 Exit` & Non-Exclusion Invariant):** `LGA-01` (`CouncilComplain`), `LGA-05` (`VitalProof`), `LGA-10` (`MarketCollect`), and `LGA-11` (`WardPHC`) MUST expose zero-data USSD (`*347*LGA#`) and 5-locale (**`en-NG`**, **`ha-NG`**, **`yo-NG`**, **`ig-NG`**, **`pcm-NG`**) SMS receipt verification alongside the \< 35 KB `KioskBase` PWA, plus a non-penalized paper-assisted counter workflow so no citizen is excluded for lacking a smartphone or NIN.  
7. **Land Use Act 1978 Section 6 Customary Right of Occupancy (Customary C of O) & `LGA-13 CustomaryDocket` Linkage:** Before an LGA registers a Customary Right of Occupancy under Section 6 of the Land Use Act 1978 or assesses `LGA-09` (`TenementRate`), the node MUST execute a `LandGuard` 3×3m polygon intersection check against `LGA-13` (`CustomaryDocket`) and the State High Court *lis pendens* registry.  
8. **The Five Tests (`01 Exit`, `02 Code`, `03 Audit`, `04 Govern`, `05 Fork`) Compliance Attestation:**  
* **`01 Exit`:** Zero-cost bulk SQLite/JSON-LD data export for any of the 774 LGAs changing TSPs; \< 60s `ClearAm` consent revocation; mandatory USSD/paper non-exclusion fallback.  
* **`02 Code`:** `NS-RFC-014` specification dedicated under **CC0-1.0 Universal Public Domain**; `@nigeriastack/lga-kiosk-kit` reference SDK licensed under **Apache-2.0 OR MIT**.  
* **`03 Audit`:** Daily signed SHA-256 Merkle roots (`/v1/audit/treasury-merkle-root` & `/v1/audit/merkle-root`) verifying zero `SJLGA` or vendor diversion under `SC/CV/343/2024`.  
* **`04 Govern`:** Governed jointly by `WG-05` & `WG-03` with ALGON (Association of Local Governments of Nigeria), State Auditors General, NFIU, and civic monitors.  
* **`05 Fork`:** Decentralized `did:web` (`https://{lga_slug}.{state}.gov.ng/.well-known/ngstack-keys.json`) key discovery allowing any LGA to self-host without central gatekeeping.
