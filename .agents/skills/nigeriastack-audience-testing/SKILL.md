# Test representative NigeriaStack journeys

- Confirm the candidate and record the SHA-256 of its serving-file receipt. Use an owner-authorised review surface and synthetic identifiers. Treat a new build as a fresh evidence target and record the scope of any retest.
- Use [scenario cards](references/journeys.md) and [the session receipt](assets/audience-receipt.json). Recruit actual or likely users for the five stated audiences. Cover their normal devices and conditions. A colleague dry run can refine the script.
- Observe the first action, navigation, completion, recovery and interpretation of the result. Record whether a moderator helped. Keep only participant aliases and de-identified findings in shared evidence. Keep any consent or raw recording in restricted storage under the arrangements for the session.
- Log each observed failure with its effect and whether it blocks a critical task. Preserve failures and attach a finding disposition and a fresh observation of the retest when fixed. Record minimum coverage of all five groups and report the limits of the observed sample.
- Use [the evidence checker](references/evidence-check.md) before handing off the findings. Only a representative human session can provide this gate's evidence. Record `PENDING` when sessions are unavailable.
